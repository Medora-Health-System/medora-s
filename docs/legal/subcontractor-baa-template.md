## 1. Purpose

Business Associate engages Subcontractor to provide [hosting / database / email / monitoring] services that may involve access to **PHI** on behalf of Business Associate's Covered Entity customers.

---

## 2. Flow-down obligations

Subcontractor agrees to same restrictions and conditions that apply to Business Associate under 45 CFR 164.502(e) and 164.504(e), including:

- Limited use/disclosure of PHI
- Appropriate safeguards (Security Rule)
- Breach reporting to Business Associate without unreasonable delay
- Subcontractor chain (no further subcontract without BA approval + agreement)
- Return/destruction of PHI at termination

---

## 3. Permitted activities

Subcontractor may access PHI only to: [describe — e.g., store encrypted database backups, serve application over TLS]

---

## 4. Audit and compliance

Business Associate may request evidence of compliance (SOC 2 report, HITRUST, questionnaire) annually.

---

## 5. Term

Co-terminous with underlying customer BAA / MSA or until Subcontractor no longer handles PHI.
