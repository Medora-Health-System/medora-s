## 1. Definitions

Terms have meanings in 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164 (HIPAA Rules).

**PHI** — Protected Health Information provided by Covered Entity or created/received by Business Associate on behalf of Covered Entity.

---

## 2. Permitted uses and disclosures

Business Associate may use/disclose PHI only to:

- Perform Services under MSA
- As required by law
- For proper management and administration (with safeguards)
- As specified in Agreement or required by BAA chain

**Minimum necessary** applies.

---

## 3. Business Associate obligations

Business Associate will:

3.1 Not use or disclose PHI other than as permitted  
3.2 Use appropriate **administrative, physical, and technical safeguards** (Security Rule)  
3.3 Report **breaches** to Covered Entity without unreasonable delay (and within [60] days / per 45 CFR 164.410)  
3.4 Ensure **subcontractors** agree to same restrictions (Subcontractor BAA)  
3.5 Make PHI available for **individual access** (45 CFR 164.524) — assist Covered Entity  
3.6 Make PHI available for **amendment** (164.526) — assist Covered Entity  
3.7 Provide **accounting of disclosures** (164.528) — assist Covered Entity  
3.8 Make internal practices available to **HHS** for compliance determination  
3.9 Return or destroy PHI at termination if feasible; if not, extend protections  
3.10 Maintain **audit controls** and access management per Security Rule  

---

## 4. Covered Entity obligations

Covered Entity will:

- Provide minimum necessary PHI
- Notify BA of permitted uses/disclosures and restrictions
- Not request BA to violate HIPAA

---

## 5. Subcontractors

BA will enter Subcontractor BAAs before subcontractors create/receive/maintain PHI on BA's behalf.

## 6. Breach notification

BA will notify CE of Breach without unreasonable delay including: nature of PHI, individuals affected (if known), mitigation, contact point.

---

## 7. Term and termination

Either party may terminate if other party materially breaches and fails to cure within [30] days. Upon termination, PHI return/destruction per §3.9.
