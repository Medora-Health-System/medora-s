## 1. Purpose

Protect patients, PHI, and platform integrity.

---

## 2. Permitted use

Authorized clinical, administrative, and billing workflows within assigned role and facility.

---

## 3. Prohibited conduct

- Accessing records without job need or valid break-glass reason
- Sharing credentials or bypassing MFA
- Exporting PHI to unauthorized systems or personal email
- Reverse engineering, penetration testing without written authorization
- Introducing malware, scraping, or automated abuse
- Using platform for non-healthcare unlawful purposes
- Circumventing audit or facility boundaries
- Storing PHI in unapproved personal cloud storage

---

## 4. Break-glass

Emergency access must document reason; sessions expire per policy; subject to audit review.

---

## 5. Monitoring

Medora and Customer may monitor usage, audit logs, and security events. No expectation of privacy in system metadata.

---

## 6. Enforcement

Violation may result in suspension, termination, and reporting to Customer compliance officer or authorities.

---

## 7. Reporting

Report suspected violations to [security@medora.health] and Customer admin.

---

## Acknowledgment

Users must acknowledge AUP at login or during onboarding.
